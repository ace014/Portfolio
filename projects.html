LIST OF OUR PROJECTS TOGETHER

Musikahan 2023 Website

Date: February 2023

During our senior high school year, we were given the opportunity by our programming teacher to develop the official website for the Musikahan Festival 2023. It was our first real-world project, and we handled everything using static web technologies—pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We even had meetings with city representatives and tourism staff to understand the content and requirements. Despite the challenge, the website was successfully published and went live during the event. It marked our first step into professional web development.

Virtual Automotive Simulator (VAM)

Date: June 2023

This was our senior high school capstone project, developed in collaboration as our final requirement. VAM is a Unity-based game/simulator designed to assist automotive teachers at our school in demonstrating concepts and procedures in a virtual environment. It was by far the most challenging and ambitious project we’ve worked on, combining multiple disciplines such as C# programming, 3D modeling, game design, and interface development. Despite time constraints, we successfully presented the first-ever capstone project in our strand and earned recognition for having the best research paper and being the “Most Innovative” project in our batch.
