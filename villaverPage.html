I am an experienced Unity game developer proficient in C#, with additional expertise in Java and SQL database management. My foundation in programming was built through years of passion and curiosity, starting in my early childhood. I became fascinated with how games worked beneath the surface, which led me to experiment with modding various games. That curiosity quickly evolved into a deeper interest in programming and development—a drive that continues to grow with every project I work on.

I have developed a strong background in full-stack web development, capable of building websites from the ground up—from planning and design to coding and deployment—using technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP. Alongside my technical expertise, I also bring creativity to the table. My experience includes music production, pixel art, and 3D texturing, all of which complement my work in game development. I am also capable of creating 3D models, producing graphic designs, and editing videos for both professional and personal projects. With a balanced skill set that bridges the technical and the artistic, I aim to deliver engaging and polished digital experiences.
